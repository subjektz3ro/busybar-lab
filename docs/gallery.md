These examples illustrate the apps; they are not live data from your bar.
Use the [quickstart](quickstart.md) to run them, or return to the
[repository overview](../README.md).

## Skystrip

Time is accelerated in these examples; the day cycle uses the public Greenwich
fixture, half an hour per frame. Decorative seasonal art follows a
northern-temperate calendar. Your live sky uses your configured location.

| Example | Preview |
|---|---|
| **Daylight** — a solstice day from dawn to dawn | ![Skystrip day cycle](media/skystrip-day.gif) |
| **Seasons** — the same time and weather through the year | ![Skystrip seasons](media/skystrip-seasons.gif) |
| **Weather** — clear, overcast, drizzle, downpour, storm, severe and snow | ![Skystrip weather](media/skystrip-weather.gif) |
| **Christmas** — decorations off and on | ![Skystrip Christmas](media/skystrip-christmas.gif) |

[Skystrip controls, geographic support and provider terms](../apps/skystrip.md).
